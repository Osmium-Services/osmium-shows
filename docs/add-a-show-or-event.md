**Osmium Events/Shows** lists the shows and events you attend.

## Add a show

1. Press **Add Show**.
2. Enter the **Show name**.
3. Enter the **Start date** and **End date**. Use the public show dates, not your setup days.
4. Enter the **Venue** and **Postcode**. The postcode is looked up when you save, to place the pin on the map.
5. Add a **More info / tickets link** if there is one.
6. Upload a **Show logo**. It uploads straight away and replaces any existing logo, and spare white space is trimmed for you.
7. Tick **Published** to show it on the site, then press **Save**.

## Show or hide one

Use **Publish** or **Hide** on a show's row. **Edit** changes its details, and **Delete** moves it to **Deleted Shows**, where **Purge All** removes them for good.

Shows that ended before the start of this year move to **Archived Shows** and are no longer listed on the site, whether or not they are ticked Published.
